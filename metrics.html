Competitiveness in the Post-CBA NBA

Although only two complete seasons have passed since the CBA's ratification, statistical evidence exists that the landscape of the NBA has, at the very least, shifted.

Large-Market Dominance Among Top Winning Teams

How has the share of large-market franchises among the league's top performers changed since the new CBA took effect?

According to regular-season win totals, during ten years prior to the 2023 CBA period (2014–2023), large market franchises accounted for an average of 62% of the top five winningest teams, despite large market teams only comprising 40% of the league. In contrast, during the post-CBA period (2024–2026), those shares have thus far fallen to roughly 33% among the top five and 46% among the top eight. In no post-CBA season has the proportion of large market teams among the top five reached even the average of the previous ten years.

The Large Markets

A look at the biggest markets in basketball.

How do we define a large market? Large markets, for the purposes of this project, are defined by population size — this measure tends to correlate with areas in which owners maintain the highest purchasing power.

Season Win Variance Across the League

Measuring the spread of team win totals reveals whether outcomes have become more or less concentrated over time.

Comparing the ten seasons preceding the 2023 CBA (2014–2023) with the post-CBA period (2024–2026), average season win variance increased from approximately 144.6 to 170.2, a rise of around 17.7%. While this increase in win dispersion appears to signal worse parity, considered alongside declining large-market dominance among the league's most successful teams, it also points toward a league in which failure is more evenly distributed among large and small-market teams and success is less concentrated with large-market teams. Importantly, moreover, the NBA from 1981 to 2019 did not see five consecutive years with different champions; however, from the 2019 season until present, the league has seen seven different winners each year.
